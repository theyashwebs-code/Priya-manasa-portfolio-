01 / ABOUT ME

Engineering quality into every experience.

A career shaped by enterprise applications, CRM platforms and a simple standard: work I would be proud to sign off.

My career began in software testing at Wipro Technologies, where I built a rigorous foundation in test planning, test case design, execution and defect management across client applications. That grounding in quality became the lens through which I approach every engagement since.

At Accenture, I progressed from application development into packaged application development, working across enterprise CRM platforms — Salesforce CRM, CPQ, CLM and nCino — for clients in banking and utilities. Today, as a Quality Engineering Team Lead, I lead quality efforts for complex enterprise engagements, from test strategy through to release.

I believe quality is engineered in, not inspected in. My work centres on clear test planning, maintainable automation, disciplined execution and honest reporting — so that releases are predictable, defects are found early, and teams deliver on time with confidence.
